About the Book

A quiet family. A buried past. A blade that was never meant to be forgotten.

Lucy thought she had left the darkness behind.

Living an ordinary life in London with her husband and children, she had no reason to believe that the past would ever find her again. Until one night, everything she loves is taken from her.

Grief turns into something colder.

As Lucy begins hunting for the truth, she discovers that the people responsible are connected to something far larger than a single tragedy — a hidden network, buried secrets, and a name that keeps appearing where it shouldn’t.

Her search takes her through London and eventually to Tokyo, where the answers become more dangerous than the questions.

But Lucy is not the only one carrying secrets.

Somewhere beneath the lies is a past that was never truly buried.

And when the truth finally surfaces, Lucy will have to decide how much of herself she is willing to lose for revenge.

THE LAST BLADE is a cinematic revenge thriller filled with mystery, action, family secrets, survival, and a past that refuses to stay dead.

About the Author

Mathavan Uma Mageshwari

Mathavan Uma Mageshwari is an independent author and storyteller with a passion for mystery, action, revenge, survival, and stories driven by human relationships.

THE LAST BLADE is the author’s debut novel, a cinematic revenge thriller exploring family, secrets, betrayal, survival, and a past that refuses to stay buried.

Through his stories, Mathavan aims to combine suspense, emotion, action, and mystery to create immersive experiences for readers. THE LAST BLADE marks the beginning of his journey as an author, with more stories to come.
