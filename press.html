Author bio

Mathavan Uma Mageshwari is an independent author and storyteller with a passion for mystery, action, revenge, survival, and stories driven by human relationships.

THE LAST BLADE is the author's debut novel, a cinematic revenge thriller exploring family, secrets, betrayal, survival, and a past that refuses to stay buried.

Through his stories, Mathavan aims to combine suspense, emotion, action, and mystery to create immersive experiences for readers.

Book description (short)

Lucy thought she had left the darkness behind — until one night, everything she loves is taken from her. Her search for the truth leads from London to Tokyo, where the answers are more dangerous than the questions.

THE LAST BLADE is a cinematic revenge thriller filled with mystery, action, family secrets, survival, and a past that refuses to stay dead.
